A single 1024×1024 infographic summing up 21 November 2026's world news: Russia firing more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight, with two missiles landing in Poland; an AI sell-off sinking US stocks, the S&P 500 down 3.7% into correction; a strong earthquake shaking the Peruvian Andes; and a trial pill (daraxonrasib) making pancreatic cancer undetectable on scans in 1 in 12 patients.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is drawn programmatically with Python and PIL: a header naming the day, and a 2×2 grid of four story panels — strikes, markets, quake, and health — each with its own accent colour, headline and short body text. `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.
